Components of the Software Development Life Cycle

👤 1. Client / Business
Defines the business problem and project goals.

📄 2. Business Requirements (BRD)
Captures what the business needs.

⚙️ 3. Functional Requirements (FRD)
Defines how the system should behave.

🏛️ 4. High-Level Design (HLD)
Designs the overall system architecture and major modules.

🛠️ 5. Low-Level Design (LLD)
Designs classes, APIs, database, and implementation details.

📊 6. UML Diagrams
Visualizes the system using:
• Use Case Diagram
• Class Diagram
• Sequence Diagram
• Activity Diagram
• Component Diagram
• Deployment Diagram

🎨 7. UI/UX Design
Creates wireframes, prototypes, and user-friendly interfaces.

💻 8. Development

Developers build the application based on approved designs.

9. Testing (QA)
Ensures the application is reliable, secure, and meets requirements.

10. User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
Business users validate that the software meets their expectations.

11. Deployment & Maintenance

Deploy the application to production, monitor it, fix bugs, and deliver future enhancements.
